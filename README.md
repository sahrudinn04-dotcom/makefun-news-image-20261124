## What it is

A newspaper front page (`entry.png`, 1024×1024) summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in six stories, each reported that day:

- **Huawei found guilty** — a US jury convicts Huawei of trade-secret theft and bank fraud, acquitting it of racketeering.
- **Four tankers hit mines** — blasts in the Strait of Hormuz; Iran rules out talks with the U.S.
- **Ukraine hits Russian ships** — drone strikes in the Gulf of Finland and off Libya.
- **Riyadh tower struck** — a Houthi drone attack wounds 11, the second hit in a week.
- **Nvidia shares slide 7%** — the rally cools a day after Jensen Huang's return as CEO.
- **Senegal win AFCON** — the Court of Arbitration for Sport restores Senegal's Africa Cup of Nations title.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the image to `/output/entry.png`. Run it locally:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
